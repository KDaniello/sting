## Why this file is not the other dataset's problem

`operation-dictionary.json` is learned from this repository's own corpora and is CC0-1.0. The two are
separate files for exactly this reason: a file whose terms depend on which entry a reader looks at is
a file nobody can use. Merging them happens in memory, at the moment a question is answered, and what
comes out of that merge is an answer rather than a dataset.

## What this is not

It is not a claim of authorship over their work. Their catalogue is theirs; what this repository
holds is what its own extractor read out of their published sources, recorded with the commit it was
read at ([`FOUNDATION-CATALOGUE-SOURCE.md`](./FOUNDATION-CATALOGUE-SOURCE.md)).
